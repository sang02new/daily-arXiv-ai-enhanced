### [1] [A Reaction-Advection-Diffusion Model to describe Non-Uniformities in Colorimetric Sensing using Thin Porous Substrates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25124)
*Kulkarni Namratha,S. Pushpavanam*

Main category: physics.flu-dyn

TL;DR: Modeling shows non-uniform, ring-like product distributions in paper-based colorimetric sensors can arise from coupled imbibition, transport, reaction and species mobility within porous substrates—even without evaporation. Key drivers include analyte–reagent ratio, substrate thickness/porosity and immobilization.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Non-uniform color patterns reduce analytical accuracy of paper-based colorimetric sensors; mechanisms beyond the coffee-ring (evaporation-driven) effect are unclear, particularly mid-radius ring formation.

Method: Two-stage axisymmetric model: Stage 1 — sessile-droplet-driven imbibition generating two flow domains with moving boundaries; Stage 2 — post-penetration transport and reaction. One reactant embedded in substrate, the other delivered by droplet. Species–substrate interactions modeled via a mobility factor. Two configurations analyzed: Reagent-Embedded (RE) and Analyte-Embedded (AE). Parameter sweeps and experimental validation with lead (RE) and nitrite (AE).

Result: Model reproduces three characteristic spatial color patterns and predicts ring formation without evaporation. Lower analyte:reagent ratios shift product inward. Thicker/more porous papers increase spatial uniformity but lower signal intensity. Immobilizing embedded species improves uniformity depending on configuration and product mobility. Multiple rings can be generated by interplay of imbibition fronts and reaction zones.

Conclusion: Transport–reaction coupling within porous substrates is sufficient to explain ring-like and multiple-ring patterns in paper sensors; model provides design insights (concentration ratios, substrate properties, immobilization) to improve uniformity and sensor performance.

Abstract: Non-uniform product (color) distribution in colorimetric paper-based sensors affects the accuracy and reliability of measurements. The underlying mechanisms responsible for this are still unclear. The coffee ring effect explains the ring-formation at the periphery. However, ring-like patterns can also be found at intermediate radial positions in these sensors. In this work, we study the influence of mass transport and reaction dynamics within porous/paper substrates on the spatial product distribution. We consider one reactant embedded in a porous substrate, which reacts with another delivered through a sessile droplet. The process is modeled in two stages. In Stage 1, droplet imbibition creates two distinct flow domains in the substrate with moving boundaries. Stage 2 commences after complete penetration. Species-substrate interactions are addressed by including a mobility factor. The developed model is used to analyze the effects of different parameters on the product distribution for two configurations, Reagent-Embedded (RE) and Analyte-Embedded (AE). Our work demonstrates ring-like patterns can form even without evaporation effects. With decreasing analyte-reagent concentration ratio, the profile shifts inward. Thicker, more porous substrates yield greater uniformity but reduce color intensity. Immobilization of embedded species enhances uniformity in RE configuration with mobile product, and in AE configuration with immobile product. The model is validated with lead and nitrite detection experiments for RE and AE configurations respectively. It successfully captures three spatial color variations observed experimentally. This study also explains the emergence of multiple rings in these systems. The insights gained are useful for optimizing sensor design and protocols for colorimetry

### [2] [A High-Order Compact Finite Volume Method for Unstructured Grids: Scheme Space Formulation and One-Dimensional Implementations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25225)
*Ling Wen,Yan-Tao Yang,Qing-Dong Cai*

Main category: physics.flu-dyn

TL;DR: 제안된 방법은 평균값과 함수값·도함수값 사이의 선형관계를 설정하고, 고차 콤팩트 재구성 설계를 미확정 동차선형계의 영공간(null space) 문제로 환원하여 '스킴 공간'을 정의한다. 이 공간에서 푸리에 해석으로 분산·소산 특성을 분석하고, WENO 개념으로 비선형 가중치를 적용해 WCFV를 구성하여 충격 포착 능력을 갖춘다.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 고차 유한체적법에서 스텐실을 변경하지 않으면서도 분산·소산 성질을 제어하고 충돌현상에서도 비진동성을 확보하는 컴팩트 재구성 기법을 단순하고 일반적으로 구성하려는 목적.

Method: 평균값↔함수값·도함수값 간 선형 근사 관계를 세우고, 계수결정 문제를 전통적 테일러 전개 대신 동차선형계의 영공간을 구하는 문제로 변환한다. 영공간을 '스킴 공간'이라 정의하고, 이 공간 내 모든 스킴의 분산·소산 특성을 푸리에 분석으로 평가한다. 다중 스텐실과 WENO 가중치를 결합하여 비선형 WCFV 스킴을 구성한다.

Result: 스킴 공간 내 여러 스킴이 동일한 정확도를 만족하지만 서로 다른 분산·소산 특성을 가짐을 보였고, 제안한 WCFV가 1차원 수치실험에서 고차 정확도, 강건성, 충격 포착 능력을 보임.

Conclusion: 스킴 공간 개념은 동일 스텐실에서 분산·소산을 능동적으로 제어할 수 있는 일반적 틀을 제공하며, WENO와 결합한 WCFV는 불연속을 안정적으로 포착하면서 고차 정확도를 유지한다.

Abstract: This paper presents a novel and straightforward compact reconstruction procedure for the high-order finite volume method on unstructured grids. In this procedure, we constructed a linear approximation relationship between the mean values and the function values, as well as the derivative values. Compared with the classical compact schemes, which employ a Taylor expansion method to determine the coefficients, our approach adopts an equivalent and more generalized method to achieve this goal. Via this method, the problem of constructing a high-order compact scheme is transformed into solving the null space of undetermined homogeneous linear systems. This null space constitutes the complete set of schemes that meet the specified accuracy under a given stencil, and is termed the 'scheme space'. Schemes within the scheme space possess the same accuracy level yet exhibit distinct dispersion and dissipation characteristics. Through Fourier analysis, we can get the dissipation and dispersion properties of all schemes in the scheme space. This facilitates the control of scheme dispersion and dissipation without altering the stencil compactness. Combined with the WENO (Weighted Essentially Non-Oscillatory) concept, multi-stencil schemes are employed to construct the nonlinear weighted compact finite volume scheme (WCFV). The WCFV is capable of eliminating unphysical oscillations at discontinuities, thereby enabling the capture of strong discontinuities. One-dimensional schemes are discussed in detail, and numerical results demonstrate that the proposed method exhibits high-order accuracy, robustness, and shock-capturing capability.

### [3] [Subject-Specific Low-Field MRI Synthesis via a Neural Operator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24968)
*Ziqi Gao,Nicha Dvornek,Xiaoran Zhang,Gigi Galiana,Hemant Tagare,Todd Constable*

Main category: eess.IV

TL;DR: 고자장(LF) MRI의 대비 저하와 낮은 SNR 문제를 해결하기 위해, 소수의 쌍으로 이루어진 고자장(HF)-저자장(LF) MRI 데이터를 학습하여 HF에서 LF로의 열화를 직접 모델링하는 엔드투엔드 합성 프레임워크(H2LO)를 제안한다. H2LO는 좌표-영상 분리 신경 연산자를 도입해 고주파 잡음 텍스처와 영상 구조를 보존하며, T1w/T2w 실험에서 기존 잡음 주입/평활화 기반 및 이미지-대-이미지 변환 모델보다 더 충실한 LF 시뮬레이션과 향상된 후속 복원 성능을 보였다.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LF MRI는 비용·접근성 이점이 있으나 SNR과 대비 열화로 임상 활용 제한. HF→LF 시뮬레이션은 장비 평가와 LF 알고리즘 개발에 유용하나 기존 시뮬레이터는 실제 대비 손실을 재현하지 못함.

Method: 소수의 페어드 HF-LF 영상을 사용해 HF→LF 열화 함수를 직접 학습. 좌표-영상(decoupled) 신경 연산자(H2LO)를 설계하여 좌표(공간적 위치)와 영상 특징을 분리 모델링하고, 고주파 잡음 텍스처 및 구조적 열화를 캡처하도록 특화.

Result: T1w 및 T2w 데이터에서 H2LO가 기존 파라메터화 잡음 합성 모델 및 인기 있는 이미지-투-이미지 변환 모델보다 더 충실한 LF 이미지를 생성. 또한 생성된 합성이 후속 이미지 향상(복원) 성능을 개선함.

Conclusion: 소수의 페어드 데이터로 실제 LF 열화를 재현할 수 있으며, 이는 LF MRI의 진단 가능성 향상과 LF 전용 알고리즘 개발에 기여할 잠재력이 있다.

Abstract: Low-field (LF) magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) improves accessibility and reduces costs but generally has lower signal-to-noise ratios and degraded contrast compared to high field (HF) MRI, limiting its clinical utility. Simulating LF MRI from HF MRI enables virtual evaluation of novel imaging devices and development of LF algorithms. Existing low field simulators rely on noise injection and smoothing, which fail to capture the contrast degradation seen in LF acquisitions. To this end, we introduce an end-to-end LF-MRI synthesis framework that learns HF to LF image degradation directly from a small number of paired HF-LF MRIs. Specifically, we introduce a novel HF to LF coordinate-image decoupled neural operator (H2LO) to model the underlying degradation process, and tailor it to capture high-frequency noise textures and image structure. Experimental results in T1w and T2w MRI demonstrate that H2LO produces more faithful simulated low-field images than existing parameterized noise synthesis models and popular image-to-image translation models. Furthermore, it improves performance in downstream image enhancement tasks, showcasing its potential to enhance LF MRI diagnostic capabilities.

### [4] [Underdetermined Blind Source Separation via Weighted Simplex Shrinkage Regularization and Quantum Deep Image Prior](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25384)
*Chia-Hsiang Lin,Si-Sheng Young*

Main category: eess.IV

TL;DR: 제안된 GQ-μ는 멀티스펙트럴 혼합 문제(MU)를 가상의 고해상도 HSI로 변환하는 Quantum Deep Image Prior(QDIP)과, HSI 픽셀의 볼록기하 구조를 이용한 가중 단체 수축(WSS) 정규화를 결합해, 본래 부정정(언더디터미넷) 문제를 과정정(오버디터미넷)으로 바꿔 풀어낸다.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: MSI는 밴드 수가 적아 MU는 BSS 관점에서 언더디터미넷 문제로 매우 어려움. 따라서 MU를 해결하기 위해 가상의 많은 밴드를 만들어 HU(오버디터미넷)로 변환하면 문제를 완화할 수 있다는 발상.

Method: 관찰된 MSI에 대해 QDIP를 통해 가상 밴드 분할(virtual band-splitting)을 수행하여 가상 HSI를 생성하고, 이 가상 HSI에 대해 HU를 수행해 가상 스펙트럼 소스들을 얻음. HU의 역문제성(ill-posed)을 완화하기 위해 HSI 픽셀의 볼록기하(convex geometry)를 이용한 가중 단체 수축(WSS) 정규화를 설계하고, 얻은 가상 스펙트럼을 스펙트럼 다운샘플링해 원래의 멀티스펙트럴 소스를 복원. 정규화 강도는 풍부도( abundance) 텐서의 희소성 패턴에 따라 적응적으로 제어.

Result: 시뮬레이션 및 실세계 데이터에서 비지도 GQ-μ가 MU 과제를 효과적으로 해결함을 보였고, 제거 실험(ablation)에서 QDIP가 고전적 DIP보다 우수하고 WSS가 메커니즘적 유효성을 제공함을 검증.

Conclusion: QDIP와 기하학 기반 WSS의 결합으로 MU 문제를 HU로 변환해 유의미한 성능 향상을 달성했으며, 향후 계산 복잡도·노이즈 민감도·실제 위성 데이터 일반화성 등의 추가 검증이 필요함.

Abstract: As most optical satellites remotely acquire multispectral images (MSIs) with limited spatial resolution, multispectral unmixing (MU) becomes a critical signal processing technology for analyzing the pure material spectra for high-precision classification and identification. Unlike the widely investigated hyperspectral unmixing (HU) problem, MU is much more challenging as it corresponds to the underdetermined blind source separation (BSS) problem, where the number of sources is larger than the number of available multispectral bands. In this article, we transform MU into its overdetermined counterpart (i.e., HU) by inventing a radically new quantum deep image prior (QDIP), which relies on the virtual band-splitting task conducted on the observed MSI for generating the virtual hyperspectral image (HSI). Then, we perform HU on the virtual HSI to obtain the virtual hyperspectral sources. Though HU is overdetermined, it still suffers from the ill-posed issue, for which we employ the convex geometry structure of the HSI pixels to customize a weighted simplex shrinkage (WSS) regularizer to mitigate the ill-posedness. Finally, the virtual hyperspectral sources are spectrally downsampled to obtain the desired multispectral sources. The proposed geometry/quantum-empowered MU (GQ-$μ$) algorithm can also effectively obtain the spatial abundance distribution map for each source, where the geometric WSS regularization is adaptively and automatically controlled based on the sparsity pattern of the abundance tensor. Simulation and real-world data experiments demonstrate the practicality of our unsupervised GQ-$μ$ algorithm for the challenging MU task. Ablation study demonstrates the strength of QDIP, not achieved by classical DIP, and validates the mechanics-inspired WSS geometry regularizer.
