### [1] [Branching Paths Statistics for confined Flows : Adressing Navier-Stokes Nonlinear Transport](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01292)
*Daniel Yaacoub,Gaëtan Brunetto,Stéphane Blanco,Richard Fournier,Gerjan Hagelaar,Jean-François Cornet,Jérémi Dauchet,Thomas Vourc'h*

Main category: physics.flu-dyn

TL;DR: 논문 초록은 연속 분기(stochastic branching) 확률적 경로-공간 표현을 비선형 대류가 존재하는 거시적 확산-대류 모델에 적용하고, 이를 Navier–Stokes의 강비선형 수송 문제로 확장해 새로운 전파자(propagator) 표현 및 후방 몬테카를로 알고리즘 가능성을 제안한다.

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 대류와 확산이 동시에 작용하는 복잡한 수송 현상(기후, 지구물리, 공학, 생체계 등)에서 기존의 수치·확률적 방법으로는 정확한 경로-공간 표현과 효율적 시뮬레이션이 어려워, 연속 분기 과정에 기반한 새로운 표현이 필요하다.

Result: Navier–Stokes의 강비선형 수송에 대한 새로운 전파자 표현을 도출하고, 밀폐된(경계가 있는) 도메인에서 유체 시뮬레이션을 위한 새로운 후방 몬테카를로 경로샘플링 방법론의 가능성을 제시한다.

Conclusion: 아이디어는 유망하지만, 엄밀한 수학적 정당화(수렴성, 경계처리, 안정성)와 수치적 검증(성능·비용 비교)이 부족하다. 향후 엄격한 증명과 광범위한 수치 실험이 필요하다.

Abstract: Recent advances have allowed to tackle exact path-space probabilistic representations of macroscopic advection-diffusion models involving advection nonlinearities by step forward approaches in terms of continuous branching stochastic processes. Yet, the need of such paradigm shift is huge for the broad flied of fluid flows. In deed, wherever for climate dynamics, engeenering, geophysical and planetary formations, or biomedical applications, complex transport phenomena involving diffusion and advection in confined domains set the physics. In this work, we advance this framework by casting such branching representations within the class of Navier-Stokes strongly nonlinear transport. This yields novel propagator representations for fluid dynamics and opens new routes for efficient simulations of fluids in confined domains by use of new Backward Monte Carlo algorithms.

*Lucie Pontiggia,Angélique Campaniello,Emmanuel Fort*

Main category: physics.flu-dyn

TL;DR: Zindler 곡선 형태의 2D 부유체를 다층 제작으로 구현해, 유체 밀도의 약 절반일 때 모든 방향에 대해 중성 평형을 보이는 것을 실험적으로 확인함. 밀도 변화를 주면 에너지 기반 모델과 일치하는 선호 방향이 나타남.

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 다층 제작 기법으로 하트형 Zindler 유사체를 제작하여 유효 밀도를 주변 유체의 약 1/2로 조정. 다양한 밀도 조건에서 회전에 대한 평형 상태를 관찰하고, 단순 에너지 기반 이론으로 결과를 해석. 밀도 불균일성과 모세관 효과 등 실제 물리적 요인의 역할을 고려.

Using a multilayer fabrication approach, we construct a heart-shaped floating object with an effective density close to one half of that of the surrounding liquid. We show experimentally that, under these conditions, the object exhibits neutral equilibrium with respect to rotation. When the density is slightly varied, preferred orientations emerge, consistent with a simple energy-based description. Our experiments highlight both the accessibility of this classical problem and the subtle role of physical effects such as density inhomogeneities and capillarity. They provide a simple platform to explore the interplay between geometry and buoyancy, and to test geometric results in a tangible setting.

### [3] [Revisiting Conservativeness in Fluid Dynamics: Failure of Non-Conservative PINNs and a Path-Integral Remedy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01968)
*Arun Govind Neelan,Ferdin Sagai Don Bosco,Naveen Sagar Jarugumalli,Suresh Balaji Vedarethinam*

Main category: physics.flu-dyn

TL;DR: The paper examines why non-conservative formulations produce wrong shock speeds and shows that standard PINNs fail for unsteady shocks due to non-vanishing viscous source terms violating Rankine–Hugoniot conditions. It proposes a path-integral (DLM) correction implemented as path-consistent losses (PI-PINN) and path-conservative schemes, recovering correct shock speeds and enabling primitive-variable PINNs for transient high-speed problems.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Clarify the longstanding conservative vs non-conservative choice in CFD and enable physically accurate primitive-variable (non-conservative) modeling in ML solvers by diagnosing the cause of shock-speed errors and proposing a fix.

Method: Use PINNs with Adaptive Weight and Viscosity (PINNs-AWV) to test shallow-water and 1D/2D Euler problems; analyze impact of viscous regularization on source terms and Rankine–Hugoniot conditions; incorporate Dal Maso–LeFloch–Murat path-integral theory to define path-consistent losses (PI-PINN) and adopt path-conservative numerical schemes.

Result: PINNs-AWV restores physical fidelity in scalar and steady problems but standard non-conservative PINNs fail in unsteady cases (e.g., Sod tube). The DLM-based path-integral correction (PI-PINN) combined with path-conservative schemes recovers correct shock speeds within non-conservative frameworks.

Conclusion: Path-integral (DLM) theory provides a rigorous mathematical correction enabling primitive-variable non-conservative formulations to be physically accurate for transient high-speed flows; recommendation to enforce path-consistency in both ML and classical solvers and to explore extensions to multi-D, complex equations, and numerical stability/efficiency.

Abstract: The choice between conservative and non-conservative formulations is a fundamental dilemma in CFD. While non-conservative forms offer intuitive modeling in primitive variables, they typically produce erroneous shock speeds. This paper critically analyzes these formulations, contrasting classical failures against the capabilities of Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs).
  Using the Adaptive Weight and Viscosity (PINNs-AWV) architecture, we evaluate cases ranging from shallow water equations to unsteady 1D and 2D Euler equations. Results reveal a significant dichotomy: while PINNs-AWV restores physical fidelity in scalar and steady systems, standard non-conservative PINNs fail in unsteady systems like the Sod shock tube. We demonstrate this failure stems from non-vanishing source terms introduced by viscous regularization, which violate the Rankine--Hugoniot jump conditions. 
  To resolve this, we implement a path-integral framework based on Dal Maso--LeFloch--Murat (DLM) theory. By incorporating path-consistent losses in PINNs (PI-PINN) and using path-conservative numerical schemes, we successfully recover correct shock speeds within non-conservative frameworks. Our results prove the path-integral approach provides a rigorous mathematical bridge for physical accuracy in both classical and machine learning solvers, enabling primitive-variable formulations in transient, high-speed simulations.

### [6] [DenOiS: Dual-Domain Denoising of Observation and Solution in Ultrasound Image Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02105)

TL;DR: DenOiS는 관측(입력)과 복원(출력) 도메인 둘 다에서 노이즈를 제거해 시뮬레이션에서만 학습해도 실제 데이터에 잘 일반화되는 초음파 속도 영상 복원 프레임워크를 제안한다.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
