For FFRDobutamine, ostial minor diameter led (AUC=0.85), followed by RI (AUC=0.83) and ILA minor diameter (AUC=0.81). RI explained 45% and 43% of FFRAdnosine and FFRDobutamine variance, respectively. At optimal thresholds, RI achieved 100% sensitivity and 95% specificity for FFRAdnosine; ostial minor diameter achieved 100% sensitivity and 57% specificity for FFRDobutamine. Conclusions: In R-AAOCA, CCTA-derived fluid dynamics-informed metrics provide excellent and superior performance compared with conventional geometric parameters in predicting hemodynamic relevance of fixed compression.
