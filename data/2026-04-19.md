### [5] [Cell-cell adhesion cannot sustain extended follower streams in a minimal non-local model of leader-follower migration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15283)
*Thomas Jun Jewell,Samuel W. S. Johnson,Ruth E. Baker,Philip K. Maini*

Main category: q-bio.CB

<details>
